# The Martians × The Book Integration

## Purpose

The Martians is the domain authority for stewardship truth. The Book is the shared proof infrastructure. Neither system replaces the other.

```text
THE MARTIANS
stewardship truth
      |
      | material signed proofs
      v
BIG BOOK
private authoritative proof history
      |
      +---- scoped participant / role verification
      |
      +---- explicit commitment or public claim
                 |
                 v
            LITTLE BOOK
            public testimony
```

## What The Martians owns

The Martians determines the meaning and validity of domain concepts such as:

- family-network identity and membership;
- relationship assertions and verification;
- contribution and stewardship;
- scoped authority;
- agreements;
- governance;
- succession;
- participant entitlement;
- enterprise relationship claims.

The Book does not decide these facts. It verifies and preserves proofs emitted under valid Martians authority.

## What the Big Book receives

Only material proof required for institutional history, rights, authority, or later verification.

A Big Book event should generally include:

- event type;
- opaque or appropriately scoped subject identifier;
- signing authority;
- privacy class;
- visibility scope;
- governing agreement/policy reference where applicable;
- digest of the exact underlying evidence relied upon;
- Vault/governed evidence reference where appropriate;
- causal/correlation lineage;
- correction, supersession, revocation, or expiration references where applicable.

The proof is not an excuse to duplicate the full family record into the ledger.

## What stays in The Vault

Examples:

- full contracts and estate documents;
- identity and government records;
- private correspondence;
- dispute evidence and deliberations;
- banking/tax source records;
- medical or child-protected records;
- recovery secrets and private keys;
- confidential enterprise documents.

## Participant proof

A participant may receive scoped verification of a record that affects their own rights without seeing the broader matter.

Example:

```text
CONTRIBUTION CLAIM
participant:9271
contribution accepted: YES
agreement reference: AG-229
entitlement reference: ENT-441
Big Book proof: ...
```

This need not expose another participant's contributions, the full agreement, family finances, or unrelated governance history.

## External authority proof

An external verifier may need to know whether a person possesses authority to act for an entity or network.

The Little Book can carry a deliberately public credential such as:

```text
AUTHORITY CLAIM
subject: X
entity/network: Y
authority: Treasury Signatory
valid: YES
expires: ...
evidence commitment: ...
Big Book state commitment: ...
issuer signature: ...
```

The verifier does not receive the governance ledger.

## Permissioned Big Book nodes

If The Book later uses a permissioned chain, useful independent node operators could include distinct trustees, auditors, custodians, trusts, or institutions with separate control.

The Martians should not claim decentralized trust merely because several nodes run under one administrator.

## Little Book prohibition

There is no `publish_family_history()`, `export_network()`, or generic Big Book projection operation.

Public testimony is claim-specific, disclosure-authorized, minimal, revocable/supersedable where appropriate, and designed so accumulated public records do not reconstruct private family history.
