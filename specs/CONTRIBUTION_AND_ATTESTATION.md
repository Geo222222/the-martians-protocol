# Contribution and Attestation Specification

## Contribution classes

The protocol recognizes that productive family value is broader than wages or cash investment. Supported contribution categories may include:

- labor;
- capital;
- intellectual property;
- enterprise creation;
- revenue creation;
- mentorship;
- caregiving;
- leadership;
- education and skill development;
- property contributed for productive use;
- opportunity creation; and
- legitimate risk bearing.

## Contribution record

A contribution should support:

- contributor MID;
- contribution type;
- recipient network, enterprise, project, or person;
- description;
- evidence references;
- event timestamp;
- optional valuation;
- verifier(s);
- verification policy;
- status; and
- protocol version.

Contribution does not automatically imply compensation, ownership, reputation, kinship, inheritance, or governance rights.

## Attestation policy engine

Verification requirements depend on the event. The protocol SHALL support configurable policies rather than one universal verifier.

Examples:

- completed software work: project owner + repository evidence;
- capital contribution: authorized financial evidence + recipient attestation;
- mentorship: recipient or guardian attestation;
- enterprise milestone: enterprise authority + objective evidence;
- high-impact event: multiple independent attestations.

Governance defines verification rules. Qualified actors verify ordinary events. Objective conditions may be machine-verified when safe.

## Immutable facts, mutable standing

Verified historical contribution remains attributable. Later misconduct does not erase earlier facts.

The protocol SHOULD model separate dimensions such as:

- lifetime contribution;
- current stewardship;
- trust;
- responsibility;
- active standing.

Corrections must supersede or annotate prior records rather than silently rewriting them.

## Reputation

Reputation may be computed from verified events, but a single opaque score must not become the source of truth. The underlying facts and dimensions should remain inspectable according to privacy rules.

Capital and token balances SHALL NOT directly purchase stewardship reputation.
