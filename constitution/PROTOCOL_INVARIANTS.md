# Protocol Invariants

These are constraints the implementation must preserve even when administrators, founders, token holders, enterprises, or voting majorities would prefer otherwise.

1. **No administrator may erase valid historical events merely because circumstances changed.** Corrections must supersede or annotate prior records rather than silently deleting history.
2. **No token holder may purchase kinship or Martian identity.**
3. **No financial contribution automatically creates stewardship reputation.**
4. **No contribution automatically creates legal ownership.**
5. **No stewardship score automatically creates ownership or unrestricted governance authority.**
6. **No family-wide vote may seize independently owned property or override external legal ownership without a valid legal mechanism.**
7. **No business governance rule automatically grants authority over another business or the family network.**
8. **No founder or elder authority is unlimited, irrevocable, or exempt from constitutional constraints.**
9. **No private source document becomes public merely because its hash or attestation is anchored on-chain.**
10. **No public blockchain shall contain raw high-risk identity, medical, tax, banking, estate, private-key, or comparable vault data.**
11. **No reputation mechanism may rewrite verified historical facts as punishment.** Current standing must be modeled separately from historical contribution.
12. **No child permanently loses identity continuity when control transitions from guardian to adult.**
13. **No single lost key should permanently destroy a critical family identity or governance function when a defined recovery policy exists.**
14. **No MARS economic rule may make purchasing MARS equivalent to joining a family network.**
15. **No protocol upgrade may silently alter the meaning of historical attestations. Version semantics must remain auditable.**
16. **No Genesis Network implementation detail may become an accidental requirement for all future family networks.**
17. **No blockchain component is mandatory where conventional cryptography and software provide a safer or simpler solution.**
18. **No token shall be issued solely to manufacture speculative demand.** Token issuance requires a documented economic function and security/legal review.
19. **No protocol treasury may spend outside its declared mandate without the governance process required by that treasury's policy.**
20. **No authority may exist without an identifiable scope, source, and succession or termination condition.**
