# Protocol Invariants

These are constraints the implementation must preserve even when administrators, founders, token holders, enterprises, family members, or voting majorities would prefer otherwise.

1. **No administrator may erase valid material historical events merely because circumstances changed.** Corrections must supersede, revoke, reverse, or annotate prior proof rather than silently rewriting history.
2. **No token holder may purchase kinship or Martian identity.**
3. **No financial contribution automatically creates stewardship reputation.**
4. **No contribution automatically creates legal ownership.**
5. **No stewardship score automatically creates ownership or unrestricted governance authority.**
6. **No family-wide vote may seize independently owned property or override external legal ownership without a valid legal mechanism.**
7. **No business governance rule automatically grants authority over another business or the family network.**
8. **No founder or elder authority is unlimited, irrevocable, or exempt from constitutional constraints.**
9. **No private source document becomes public merely because its hash, digest, commitment, or attestation is recorded.**
10. **No raw `SECRET_REGULATED` source data — including high-risk identity, medical, tax, banking, estate, child-protected, recovery-secret, private-key, or comparable material — may be stored directly in an immutable blockchain or public proof payload.**
11. **No reputation mechanism may rewrite verified historical facts as punishment.** Current standing must be modeled separately from historical contribution.
12. **No child permanently loses identity continuity when control transitions from guardian to adult.**
13. **No child, successor, guardian, or family member receives unrestricted historical access merely because of status or succession.** Sensitive history remains scoped by rights, role, matter, and legitimate need.
14. **No single lost key should permanently destroy a critical family identity or governance function when a defined recovery policy exists.**
15. **No MARS economic rule may make purchasing MARS equivalent to joining a family network.**
16. **No protocol upgrade may silently alter the meaning of historical attestations.** Version semantics must remain auditable.
17. **No Genesis Network implementation detail may become an accidental requirement for all future family networks.**
18. **No blockchain component is mandatory where conventional cryptography, access-controlled storage, and software provide a safer or simpler solution.**
19. **No token shall be issued solely to manufacture speculative demand.** Token issuance requires a documented economic function and security/legal review.
20. **No protocol treasury may spend outside its declared mandate without the governance process required by that treasury's policy.**
21. **No authority may exist without an identifiable scope, source, and succession, expiration, revocation, or termination condition.**
22. **Everything material must be provable; not every interaction, thought, communication, draft, or private fact must become an immutable event.**
23. **The Martians does not operate a separate blockchain merely because stewardship history exists.** Material Martians proofs belong in the shared private Big Book under scoped authority.
24. **Family membership does not imply blanket Big Book visibility.** Access is least-privilege and scoped by role, domain, matter, participant rights, delegated authority, and legitimate need.
25. **A participant's right to verify their own contribution, vote, agreement, authority, or entitlement does not grant access to another participant's equivalent record.**
26. **The public Little Book is not a mirror, export, replica, or readable projection of the private Big Book.**
27. **No automatic Big Book-to-Little Book publication path is permitted.** Every public claim requires explicit disclosure authority and minimum-necessary content.
28. **The Little Book must never be sufficient to reconstruct private family history**, including private relationships, disputes, contribution amounts, inheritance planning, internal standing, children's records, succession deliberations, or confidential agreements unless a specific fact is deliberately published.
29. **The Vault and governed private stores retain source evidence according to access and retention policy.** The Big Book normally preserves the digest/reference proving which exact source record was relied upon.
30. **Immutable event history must not be confused with immutable personal data.** The protocol must preserve correction and institutional provenance without unnecessarily making sensitive personal details permanent.
31. **Shared Book infrastructure never implies shared governance or cross-network privilege.** Every Family Network retains sovereign membership, authority, privacy, treasury, and governance boundaries.
32. **Public proof must establish a legitimate claim without exposing the institution beyond what the verifier needs to know whenever feasible.**
