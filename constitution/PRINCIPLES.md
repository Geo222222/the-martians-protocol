# Constitutional Principles

## Mission

The Martians Protocol enables a family to operate as a durable multi-generational institution by coordinating identity, relationships, contribution, agreements, enterprise activity, authority, institutional knowledge, and stewardship across generations while preserving the privacy and dignity of participants.

## Foundational separations

The protocol SHALL distinguish:

1. identity;
2. kinship;
3. contribution;
4. stewardship and reputation;
5. capital;
6. legal ownership;
7. governance authority; and
8. protocol economic capacity.

No implementation may treat these as interchangeable merely for convenience.

## Minimum necessary evidence

> **Everything material must be provable. Not everything known by the institution must be permanently recorded or disclosed.**

The protocol SHALL preserve enough proof to establish material history, rights, authority, governance, and accountability while minimizing unnecessary capture and exposure of private information.

## Institutional continuity

The protocol SHALL preserve valid material historical facts rather than silently rewriting history when relationships, roles, authority, or standing change.

Corrections, reversals, revocations, and superseding decisions SHALL create new proof. Institutional continuity does not require every sensitive source detail to become immutable.

## Family before token

The protocol SHALL optimize for durable family coordination, stewardship, enterprise creation, accountability, succession, privacy, and institutional memory. Token economics SHALL remain subordinate to those objectives.

## Legal reality

The protocol records, verifies, coordinates, and attests. It does not unilaterally replace applicable law, corporate records, estate instruments, title systems, or legally binding ownership documents.

## Privacy by design

The protocol SHALL enforce minimum disclosure and least privilege.

No participant receives more information than is necessary to exercise their rights, perform delegated authority, satisfy a legitimate duty, participate in a defined matter, or verify a legitimate claim.

The protocol recognizes four privacy classes:

- `PUBLIC_PROOF`;
- `PARTICIPANT_PROOF`;
- `CONFIDENTIAL_EVIDENCE`;
- `SECRET_REGULATED`.

Raw `SECRET_REGULATED` source material SHALL NOT be written directly to immutable blockchain or public proof payloads.

## The Vault, Big Book, and Little Book

The Vault SHALL hold governed source evidence requiring confidentiality, retention control, or restricted handling.

The Big Book SHALL preserve the private authoritative proof history shared across institutional domains.

The Little Book SHALL carry deliberately public testimony derived from explicit disclosure decisions and cryptographic commitments.

**The Little Book SHALL NOT be sufficient to reconstruct the Big Book.**

## Scoped authority

Authority SHALL be domain-specific. Family governance does not automatically confer authority over independently owned enterprises, personal property, trusts, or individual rights.

Access to institutional history is likewise scoped. Family membership does not automatically confer unrestricted visibility.

## Founder survivability

The system SHALL be capable of surviving its founder, administrators, current software implementation, any single generation, and any particular blockchain or infrastructure vendor.

## Contribution integrity

Verified contribution SHALL remain historically attributable even if the contributor's later standing changes.

A participant may be entitled to verify their own contribution or entitlement without receiving visibility into another participant's private record.

## Economic integrity

Capital SHALL NOT purchase kinship, historical contribution, or stewardship reputation. Stewardship SHALL NOT automatically create ownership. Ownership SHALL NOT automatically create family-wide constitutional authority.

## Protocol neutrality between families

Genesis Network 0001 serves as the first implementation, not as a permanent privileged network. Future family networks SHALL be able to operate independently under the protocol's shared standards.

Shared Book infrastructure SHALL NOT imply shared governance, membership, treasury, or cross-network read authority.
